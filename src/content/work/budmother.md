## Budmother Shopify Theme Delivery

I contributed to product-card and quick-buy implementation work for Budmother, with supporting wishlist and PDP interaction updates.

#### Project details

**Delivery type:** Iterative enhancement

- Built quick-buy and product-card flows, with reusable component logic for variants, quantity controls, and add-to-cart behavior
- Implemented quantity-adjust and form-layout behavior with matching CSS/JS updates in compiled storefront assets
- Added wishlist page/icon support and tuned related products/product sticker behaviors
- Delivered social-sharing and PDP presentation refinements, including sold-out states and icon scaling adjustments
