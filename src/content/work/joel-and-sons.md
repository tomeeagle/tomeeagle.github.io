## Joel & Son Fabrics Shopify Work

### Challenge
Joel & Son Fabrics needed a more reliable quick-add journey that worked consistently across mobile devices, translations, and product-card contexts.

### Approach
- Reworked quick-add modal behavior across product cards and modal interactions.
- Improved accessibility and translation handling during the quick-add refactor.
- Fixed iPhone viewport and close-icon visibility issues that impacted modal usability.
- Tuned quick-add placement and mobile control styling to align with desktop patterns.

### Outcome
Customers received a smoother browse-to-bag flow on mobile, with more predictable quick-add behavior across product listing experiences.

### Stack
Shopify Liquid, JavaScript, modal UX, accessibility-minded frontend implementation.
