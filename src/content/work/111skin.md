## 111SKIN Shopify Theme Development

### Challenge
111SKIN needed a steady delivery stream for merchandising and PDP improvements while keeping the theme stable through frequent release cycles.

### Approach
- Implemented PDP and collection enhancements, including savings and gift-set tag logic and product-card pricing behavior.
- Built and iterated custom sections such as product tabs, overlap hero modules, carousels, and concerns-focused content blocks.
- Delivered performance-oriented updates, including preload/fetchpriority and media attribute tuning.
- Updated snippets and supporting assets for rewards and app-facing integrations.

### Outcome
The team gained a flexible merchandising foundation and a faster path to launch campaign content without repeatedly rebuilding core theme components.

### Stack
Shopify Liquid, JavaScript, Shopify theme sections/snippets, performance optimization.
