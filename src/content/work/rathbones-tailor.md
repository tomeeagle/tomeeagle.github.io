## Rathbones Tailor Shopify Improvements

### Challenge
Rathbones Tailor's rental model introduced complex cart rules around duration conflicts and damage cover, creating avoidable friction at checkout.

### Approach
- Implemented rental-duration conflict handling with clearer modal flows and improved error states.
- Added nested cart-line support for damage-cover products and enforced line-item ordering for waiver visibility.
- Refined rental-duration and damage-cover selectors with safer defaults and clearer choices.
- Resolved iOS dropdown rendering edge cases and stabilized cart/product interactions across JS theme updates.

### Outcome
Rental-specific cart interactions became more predictable for shoppers, reducing confusion and supporting a smoother path to checkout.

### Stack
Shopify Liquid, JavaScript, cart logic customization, cross-device UX fixes.
