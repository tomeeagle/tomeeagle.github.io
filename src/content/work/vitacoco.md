## Product detail page refresh

I collaborated with an ecommerce agency to refresh this Shopify PDP experience and improve product selection clarity on mobile and desktop.

### Challenge
The existing PDP needed a clearer conversion flow, especially for variant selection and add-to-cart visibility on smaller screens.

### Approach
- Reworked PDP layout and UI details to match updated Figma designs.
- Implemented variant pickers with image swatches for clearer flavor/product differentiation.
- Added sticky add-to-cart behavior to keep primary purchase action visible during scroll.

### Outcome
The updated PDP improved selection clarity and reduced purchase friction by keeping key decision controls and CTAs easier to access.

### Stack
Shopify Liquid, JavaScript, PDP UX optimization, responsive frontend implementation.
