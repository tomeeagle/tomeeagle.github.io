## Naomi Whittel Shopify Store Enhancements

### Challenge
Naomi Whittel required a new storefront build with campaign-ready PDP storytelling, reusable content systems, and consistent UI behavior across templates.

### Approach
- Implemented custom sections for ingredients, comparison storytelling, reviews, and video testimonials.
- Built and refactored reusable review-card patterns with carousel interactions across key product templates.
- Introduced CSS variable-driven styling to standardize typography, spacing, and theme consistency.
- Shipped iterative acquisition-focused PDP refinements as campaign needs evolved.

### Outcome
The team received a scalable theme foundation that made launching new product narratives and testimonial-driven content substantially faster.

### Stack
Shopify Liquid, JavaScript, CSS architecture, modular section development.
