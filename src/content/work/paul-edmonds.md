## Paul Edmonds Shopify Development

I delivered targeted Shopify enhancements for Paul Edmonds focused on salon-specific functionality and content-heavy pages.

#### Project details

**Delivery type:** Iterative enhancement

- Implemented Google opening-hours and contact-page improvements with associated settings/schema updates
- Fixed slider arrow regressions and completed responsive spacing refinements for stylist and service content blocks
